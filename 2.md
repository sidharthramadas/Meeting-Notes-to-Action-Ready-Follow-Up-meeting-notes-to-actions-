# Meeting Notes to Action-Ready Follow-Up

Convert messy meeting notes into a scannable follow-up that anyone on the team can act on immediately.

## Output Format
1. **TL;DR (1–2 sentences):** The main outcome of the meeting.
2. **Decisions Made:** Bullet list of what was actually agreed upon. Skip small talk and side debates.
3. **Action Items (Table):** Columns for `Task`, `Owner`, and `Deadline`.
4. **Open Questions / Blockers:** Anything unresolved that still needs an answer.

## Rules
- **Never invent owners or deadlines.** If a task has no clear owner or due date in the notes, write `[TBD]` so the team catches it.
- **Keep it concise.** Strip filler, tangents, and repeated points.
- **Flag ambiguity.** If two people disagreed and no decision was reached, place it under **Open Questions**, not **Decisions Made**.
- **Tone:** Neutral, direct, and ready to paste into Slack or email.
